Shipping & Returns

Shipping

  • Free standard shipping on all orders over €100.
  • Standard delivery: 3–5 business days within the EU, 5–10 business days internationally.
  • Express delivery available at checkout for an additional fee (1–2 business days within the EU).
  • Orders are processed within 24 hours on business days.
  • A tracking number is sent by email as soon as your order ships.

Returns

  • Returns are accepted within 30 days of delivery.
  • Items must be unworn, unwashed, and returned with all original tags attached.
  • Final sale items and personalized pieces are not eligible for return.
  • To start a return, contact our client care team with your order number.
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days of receiving the return.

Exchanges

If you need a different size or color, we recommend placing a new order and returning the original item for a refund, to ensure the fastest turnaround.

Customs & Duties

International orders may be subject to customs duties and taxes, which are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the item price or shipping cost.
