Privacy Policy

BENZCOMILANO respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address and shipping details when you create an account, place an order, or subscribe to our newsletter.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process orders, provide customer support, and — with your consent — send you marketing communications about new collections and offers.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time, and can request access to or deletion of your personal data by contacting our client care team.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep your bag and wishlist up to date and to understand how our site is used, so we can improve it over time.
